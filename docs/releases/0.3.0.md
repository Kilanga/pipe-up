## Pipe Up 0.3.0

**New: display and reading options** (Settings → *Display and reading*, all off by default, applied at once in the call panel and the side panel)
- **Easy reading:** more space between letters and lines, larger text, and shorter replies (8 words at most). Helpful with dyslexia or when reading under pressure.
- **One suggestion at a time:** only the best reply is shown; key 1 picks it.
- **Hear the suggestions:** read aloud in your own headphones only, never into the call (local Piper voice if set up, otherwise a voice that runs on your computer).
- **High contrast:** black and white with a yellow highlight. Labels never rely on color alone.
- **Reduce motion:** no transitions; the system setting is always followed.

**Fixed**
- The hosted demo showed the word "null" in its Live and Settings tabs.

**Install:** download `pipe-up-extension.zip`, unzip, open `chrome://extensions`, enable Developer mode, *Load unpacked* (remove the previous version first).
