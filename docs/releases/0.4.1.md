## Pipe Up 0.4.1

**The voice works right after install.**

**Voice**
- The default voice is now included: it works right after install, with nothing to download. The two other voices remain optional downloads of about 64 MB each.
- The output still starts on "Chat only": you choose "Chat + synthetic voice" or "Synthetic voice only" in the call panel when you want the voice.
- The included voice is checked (SHA-256) each time it is loaded. If its file is missing or damaged, Pipe Up says so and keeps working in the chat.
- Coming from 0.4.0: the copy of the default voice you downloaded is removed, since it is no longer needed. The other voices you downloaded are kept.
- In Meet, the voice can be mixed into your microphone from the first call, without reloading the page after the side panel opens.
- The zip is about 64 MB (it was about 5.5 MB): almost all of it is the voice.

**Install:** download `pipe-up-extension.zip`, unzip, open `chrome://extensions`, enable Developer mode, *Load unpacked* (remove the previous version first).

Details of the bundled components and of the voices, with the license of the included voice: THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.
