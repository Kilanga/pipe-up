## Pipe Up 0.4.0

**Cloud before the call, device during the call.**

**New**
- **Prepare for the call** (Briefing tab): turn your briefing into up to 6 likely questions, each with a short answer you can edit, add to or delete.
  - *Prepare with the cloud* shows what will be sent and asks you to confirm. It makes one request and does not turn on the cloud for the call.
  - *Prepare on this computer* does the same with Gemini Nano, part by part, with progress and a *Stop* button. Nothing is sent.
  - A prepared answer that contains a number, a link or an e-mail address absent from your briefing is discarded.
  - During the call, a matching prepared answer is offered as a reply, labelled *From your briefing*.
  - Every preparation appears in the routing log (Live tab) with the route really taken.
- **Raise your hand with "I'm still speaking"** (Settings, off by default, extension only): Alt+1 can also raise your hand in Meet. Not yet verified in a live call.
- Hosted demo: a new step, **2. Prepare the answers**, on Sam's sample briefing.

**Faster**
- On your device, suggestions start during the silence before the pause and are shown at the pause if nobody spoke again. This never happens on the cloud route. Remote Config: `speculate_ms` (500 by default, 0 turns it off).
- A request replaced by a newer question is now really cancelled, on device and in the cloud.
- Not yet measured on a real Gemini Nano: no speed figure is claimed for this version.

**Voice**
- Three English voices to choose from, all trained on public-domain recordings: a female voice (default), a lower female voice and a male voice. About 64 MB each, downloaded once when you choose it.
- **Set up your voice again:** the voice of versions 0.2.0 to 0.3.1 was limited to non-commercial use. It is removed.
- Your quick phrases (Alt+1, Alt+2, Alt+3) and your Alt+D line are synthesized in advance, so they are spoken without waiting.
- Even volume (peaks at -3 dBFS). Muting in Meet silences the voice at once. A reply the voice cannot speak goes to the chat.
- The voice runs in its own worker. The extension zip went from 13.7 MB to about 5.5 MB.

**Safer**
- Hosted demo: Google reCAPTCHA is loaded only when a cloud request is first made, not when the page opens. The demo is served with security headers (content security policy, no framing, no referrer).
- Speakers' names never reach the cloud: they are replaced by "Speaker A", "Speaker B" and "Me".
- Local history: a reply you send is kept with up to 20 key words of the line it answered, never the sentence. The same key words, with the suggestions shown, are kept when you press 4 or Esc. Numbers (a phone or account number, an amount) are never kept as key words.
- Replies and prepared answers are checked before they are shown: cut at 160 characters, and dropped if they hold a link or an e-mail address that is not in your briefing. *From your briefing* is shown only when the reply shares words with your briefing and every number in it is there.
- In Meet, the page cannot reach the panel's content (closed shadow root), keys and clicks made by a script are ignored, and text a script puts into the field where you edit a reply is rejected. The page can no longer tell Pipe Up that the voice failed. Known limit: a script of the page can still ask the browser whether a given text is displayed.
- Voices come from a fixed revision of `rhasspy/piper-voices` and are checked (SHA-256) before use.
- The App Check debug token is no longer printed in the side panel's console.

**More reliable**
- A Gemini Nano call that never answers blocks one question only, not the ones that follow.
- *Prepare with the cloud*: a long briefing (more than about 1,600 words) is prepared in the cloud only. If the cloud cannot answer, you are told you can prepare on this computer. A short briefing may still be prepared by Gemini Nano when the cloud cannot be reached; the routing log says which.
- If the cloud's safety check (reCAPTCHA) is blocked, the request stops after 10 seconds with a clear message, and *Stop* works at once.
- "Voice only": if the side panel closes before the voice is ready, your reply goes to the chat.
- Coming from 0.3.1 with a voice output chosen: Pipe Up goes back to "Chat only" until you set up a voice again.

**Easier to use**
- Enter and Tab act on the selected reply only when the focus is on it (or nowhere): on another button of the panel they do what that button does.
- *Easy reading* now spaces all text, including replies, quick phrases, buttons and fields. *High contrast* is complete in the side panel. *Text size* applies at once.
- The field where you edit a reply and the prepared questions grow with their text.
- Screen readers: keys are declared as shortcuts, the notice area stays in the page so that its messages can be announced, and each "Delete" button in Memory says what it deletes. Not yet checked by hand with NVDA or VoiceOver.
- Hosted demo on small screens: the panel is brought into view when the call starts.
- Clearer words: "Don't learn from my replies", "Computer voice in the call", and one calm sentence when the cloud cannot answer.

**Install:** download `pipe-up-extension.zip`, unzip, open `chrome://extensions`, enable Developer mode, *Load unpacked* (remove the previous version first).
