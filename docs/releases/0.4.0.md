## Pipe Up 0.4.0

**Cloud before the call, device during the call.**

**New**
- **Prepare for the call** (Briefing tab): turn your briefing into up to 6 likely questions, each with a short answer you can edit, add to or delete.
  - *Prepare with the cloud* shows what will be sent and asks you to confirm. It makes one request and does not turn on the cloud for the call.
  - *Prepare on this computer* does the same with Gemini Nano, part by part, with progress and a *Stop* button. Nothing is sent.
  - A prepared answer that contains a number absent from your briefing is discarded.
  - During the call, a matching prepared answer is offered as a reply, labelled *From your briefing*.
  - Every preparation appears in the routing log (Live tab) with the route really taken.
- **Raise your hand with "I'm still speaking"** (Settings, off by default, extension only): Alt+1 can also raise your hand in Meet. Not yet verified in a live call.
- Hosted demo: a new step, **2. Prepare the answers**, on Sam's sample briefing.

**Faster**
- On your device, suggestions start during the silence before the pause and are shown at the pause if nobody spoke again. This never happens on the cloud route. Remote Config: `speculate_ms` (500 by default, 0 turns it off).
- A request replaced by a newer question is now really cancelled, on device and in the cloud.
- Not yet measured on a real Gemini Nano: no speed figure is claimed for this version.

**Voice**
- Three English voices to choose from, all trained on public-domain recordings: a female voice (default), a lower female voice and a male voice. About 64 MB each, downloaded once when you choose it.
- **Set up your voice again:** the voice of versions 0.2.0 to 0.3.1 was limited to non-commercial use. It is removed.
- Voices come from a fixed revision of `rhasspy/piper-voices` and are checked (SHA-256) before use.
- Your quick phrases (Alt+1, Alt+2, Alt+3) and your Alt+D line are synthesized in advance, so they are spoken without waiting.
- Even volume (peaks at -3 dBFS). Muting in Meet silences the voice at once. A reply the voice cannot speak goes to the chat.
- The voice runs in its own worker. The extension zip went from 13.7 MB to 5.5 MB.
