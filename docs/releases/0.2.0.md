**Pipe Up 0.2.0** — a backup channel for people who stutter in Google Meet, on-device first.

**Try it without installing anything:** https://pipe-up-demo.web.app — *Load Sam's sample briefing*, then *Start the call*. No Gemini Nano on your computer? Click *Try with the cloud*.

### New
- **Hold the floor:** Alt+1 posts "I'm still speaking, one moment." Optional disclosure line in your own words (Alt+D, empty by default).
- **On-demand mode** (Alt+S) and **edit before sending** (Tab).
- **Voice into the call:** a local Piper voice, generated on your computer and mixed into your Meet microphone only while it speaks (Live tab → *Set up the voice*, about 60 MB once).
- **Streaming:** the first reply appears about a second after the pause on Gemini Nano.
- **Routing log:** where each suggestion was generated (Firebase AI Logic `inferenceSource`) and why.
- Remote Config `prompt_version` selects a prompt variant; limited-use App Check tokens.

### Fixed (from a full code review)
Stale suggestions could be sent; learning used the wrong context; cloud fallback stayed on-device after a Nano failure; Meet keys (Enter/Esc) and IME input were intercepted; long captions could overflow the on-device prompt; no remote script in the extension any more; one side panel per Meet tab; more robust Meet chat sending; accessibility of the panel and tabs.

### Install
1. Download `pipe-up-extension.zip` below and unzip it.
2. Open `chrome://extensions`, turn on **Developer mode**, click **Load unpacked** and pick the folder.
3. Join a Meet call, turn on captions (CC), click the Pipe Up icon, then **Set up on-device AI**.

Requirements for on-device AI: Chrome desktop 139+, about 22 GB free disk space, a GPU with more than 4 GB of VRAM.

The optional voice module bundles separately licensed components (espeak-ng, GPL-3.0): see THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.
